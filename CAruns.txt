I chose rule 30 and rule 90 to compare because they produce very different
visual patterns, both starting from the seed. Rule 30
creates a chaotic, random pattern on the left side while still
following a deterministic rule. Rule 90, on the other hand, produces a
clean, self-similar  triangle, showing how a simple local
rule can generate a highly ordered, recursive structure. Comparing
them side by side highlights how sensitive these systems are to the specific
rule chosen, even though the setup is identical.
